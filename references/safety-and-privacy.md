# Safety and Privacy

## Consent and Scope

Profiling is allowed only for a consenting subject or under an explicitly
authorized and lawful process. State the purpose, sources, storage location,
audience, retention period, and deletion route before collecting sensitive
information.

The subject may:

- skip any question;
- correct or delete a conclusion;
- stop the process at any time;
- restrict the profile to conversation-only use;
- request deletion of stored profile files.

## Data Minimization

Collect only information needed for the stated purpose. Do not request contact
details, identity documents, precise location, financial records, credentials,
medical records, legal records, or intimate details unless they are essential
and explicitly authorized.

Avoid inferring protected traits or making claims about any of the following:

- health or diagnosis;
- disability;
- race or ethnicity;
- religion or belief;
- political affiliation;
- sexuality or gender identity;
- immigration or legal status;
- criminal history;
- financial worth;
- intelligence, integrity, or moral worth.

## Prohibited Uses

Never use the profile to automate, recommend, or materially influence:

- hiring, promotion, tenure, or dismissal;
- school admissions;
- credit, insurance, or housing decisions;
- clinical diagnosis or treatment;
- legal or disciplinary outcomes;
- rankings, prizes, or awards;
- any other high-impact decision about a person.

Do not create a composite score. Do not present uncertainty as certainty.

## Sensitive Material

If sensitive material appears:

1. Stop and check whether it is necessary for the stated purpose.
2. Offer to exclude it or keep it only in the current conversation.
3. Do not write it to a repository, log, example, or public artifact.
4. If stored locally, use a private location controlled by the subject.
5. Record only the minimum metadata needed to explain why it was excluded.

If the conversation indicates imminent risk of harm, stop profiling. Encourage
the person to contact appropriate local emergency, crisis, or clinical support.

## Repository Hygiene

- Keep real profiles outside version control.
- Use synthetic or fully de-identified examples in the project.
- Do not include names, email addresses, phone numbers, account IDs, or
  locations in committed examples.
- Review profile files before sharing or exporting them.
- Never upload personal data to a third-party service without explicit,
  specific authorization.

## Third-Party Profiling

A profile of someone not present is speculative. Use it only when the subject
has consented or when the task is an authorized, non-consequential review.
Label it as an inference from limited evidence and do not treat it as a finding
about the person.

## Language to Avoid

Avoid:

- "This is who you are."
- "You are naturally..."
- "This proves that..."
- "Your score means..."
- "People like you usually..."

Prefer:

- "The available evidence currently suggests..."
- "A competing explanation is..."
- "This pattern appears in context X but not yet in Y."
- "Confidence is medium because..."
- "The next observation that would test this is..."
